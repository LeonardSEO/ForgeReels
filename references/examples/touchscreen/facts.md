# Facts: how a touchscreen finds your finger

| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| Phone touchscreens are projected-capacitive: a grid of transparent electrodes (rows and columns) under the cover glass | Wikipedia, "Touchscreen", section "Projected capacitance" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Touchscreen#Projected_capacitance) |
| Each row/column crossing forms a capacitor; a finger (a conductor) draws charge and lowers the mutual capacitance at the crossings near it | same section ("mutual capacitance") |
| A controller chip measures every crossing repeatedly and reports the location with the largest change | same section |

No numbers are spoken or shown.
