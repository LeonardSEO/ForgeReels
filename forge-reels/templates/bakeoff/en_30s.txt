Most people check their phone ninety-six times a day.
But only three of those checks actually matter.
Here is the trick that changed how I work.
First, turn off every notification except calls.
Second, keep one app on your home screen: your calendar.
Third, check messages at ten, two, and five. Nothing in between.
After one week, I got back almost two hours a day.
Try it for seven days, and tell me what you did with the time.
