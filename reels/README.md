# reels/

One folder per video: `reels/<yyyy-mm-dd>-<slug>/`. Claude creates it when you start a video with `/forge-reels`
and keeps everything for that video inside it: brief, sources, script, voice (`vo/`), scenes, renders (`out/`),
QC sheets and notes. Re-open a video later by pointing Claude at its folder.

Your reels stay on your machine: everything in here except this file is ignored by git.
